# Finding gaps in the draft

A gap is anything the draft decides by default rather than by the user. Scan for each kind:

For the uncovered-cases list below, check every line against the draft one at a time — an overall impression scan misses real gaps even when the list is right there; a dry run's "done" verdict on two separate passes each missed cases a line-by-line check then caught.

- **Unreasoned values:** a token, size, or rule with no round or section-1 reason next to it.
- **Contradictions:** two picks that can't both hold (a see-through bar that must also meet a contrast rule; "one accent" plus a second colored link style).
- **Broken rules:** a pick that breaks the draft's own rules or banned list without the rule being narrowed.
- **Uncovered cases:** situations no round showed. The usual ones:
  - dark mode (does it exist, how is it switched, does every pair still meet contrast);
  - phone navigation (tab bar, back, where the hero screen's controls go);
  - the type scale (how many steps, the smallest size, what the body size is on a phone);
  - more items than the palette covers (a seventh category, a fifth speaker, a long name);
  - empty, loading, and error states;
  - status colors (warning, ok, danger) and how they look in light and dark;
  - icons (which set, which weight, what size in each context, where words replace them);
  - long content (a very long title, a very long list, a very long page);
  - tap target size, and the desktop layout if there is one;
  - motion (what moves, and what happens with reduced motion).

Ask about the gaps you found, grill-style, with your recommended answer. Show visual gaps in the mock, since the user reacts better to seeing a gap than to reading about it.
